OVERCOME DEPRESSION

What is Depression?

Depression is a mental health condition that makes a person feel sad, hopeless, or tired for a long time. It is more than just having a bad day.A teenager with depression may lose interest in things they enjoy, avoid friends, have trouble sleeping, or find it hard to focus at school. Depression can happen because of stress, difficult experiences, or sometimes for no clear reason. It is not a weakness, and it can be treated with the right support and help.

Causes

Teenage depression is rarely caused by just one single thing; instead, it usually happens when a mix of biology, stressful life events, and personal traits all pile up together

Effects

Depression causes profound changes in a teenager's mood, behavior, body, and daily life, often showing up as intense irritability and anger rather than just sadness

Help

To help with depression, start by listening without judgment and connecting them with professional support, such as a doctor, counselor, or local mental health helpline.
