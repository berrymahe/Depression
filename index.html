OVERCOME DEPRESSION

What is Depression?

is a real medical illness, not just a bad mood or regular teen angst. It makes you feel very sad, empty, or angry for a long time.

Causes

depression is believed to result from the interaction of neurobiological, genetic and environmental factors.

Effects

attitude and behavior that can cause significant distress and problems at school or home, in social activities, or in other areas of life.
