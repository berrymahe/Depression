Help with Depression

How to Communicate

  • Create a safe space: Let them talk openly without fear of getting punished or dismissed.

  • Avoid minimizing: Do not write it off as just "teenage angst" or a bad mood.

  • Stay connected: Keep inviting them to join family meals or simple outings, even if they say no at first.

Seek Professional Help

  • Talk to a doctor: Start by booking an appointment with a general practitioner (GP) or pediatrician.

  • Explore therapy: Evidence-based treatments like Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) help teens change negative thought patterns.

  • Use local helplines: In New Zealand, you can reach out or point your teen to free services like 1737 (call or text) to speak with a trained counselor anytime. If there is an immediate safety risk,contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital emergency department

    Support Daily Habits

  • Encourage movement: Suggest short walks, stretching, or physical activities they used to enjoy.

  • Promote healthy routines: Help them maintain a regular sleep schedule and eat balanced food
