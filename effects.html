Effects of Depression

Emotional and Psychological Effects

  • Persistent Low Mood: Feeling sad, empty, hopeless, or tearful nearly every day.

  • Loss of Interest: Losing pleasure in hobbies, social activities.

  • Cognitive Difficulties: Experiencing trouble concentrating, remembering details, making decisions, or feeling slowed down in thought.

  • Negative Self-Perception: Harboring intense feelings of worthlessness, excessive guilt, or helplessness.

Physical Effects on the Body

  • Fatigue and Low Energy: Feeling profoundly tired so that even small tasks require major effort.

  • Sleep Disturbances: Suffering from insomnia, waking too early, or sleeping much more than usual.

  • Appetite and Weight Changes: Experiencing a reduced appetite with weight loss or increased food cravings with weight gain.

  • Unexplained Pain: Developing chronic headaches, stomachaches, digestive problems, or general body aches without a clear medical cause

    Behavioral and Social Effects

  • Social Withdrawal: Isolating from friends, family, and community networks.

  • Work and School Disruption: Struggling with poor performance, missed attendance, or a drop in productivity.

  • Irritability and Agitation: Displaying frequent angry outbursts, frustration over minor matters, or restlessness.

  • Substance Use: Turning to alcohol or recreational drugs as a form of coping.
